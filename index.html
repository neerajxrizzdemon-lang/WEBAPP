What this demo shows

A static, client-side tracker that visualizes water usage from user input. It estimates totals and offers simple suggestions — good for explaining water conservation concepts at a school expo.

Quick facts

  • Average household uses ~100–300 L per person/day (varies by country)
  • Showering & toilet use are major contributors
  • Small changes (shorter showers, efficient taps) can save big
